Who We Are

Nadia Store is the leading online marketplace in South Asia, empowering tens of thousands of sellers to connect with millions of customers. We provide immediate and easy access to 10 million products in more than 100+ categories and deliver more than 2 million packages every month to all corners of our countries.

Nadia Store is a mall, marketplace, and community for its customers. It is also a university for entrepreneurs, and every month we educate more than 5,000 new sellers on e-commerce operations.

To overcome the logistics challenges in our market, Nadia Store has built its own logistics company specifically designed for e-commerce operations - Nadia Express (known as NEX) - to raise the standards for the industry. We are also helping existing and new logistics providers become digitized.

In 2018, Nadia Store was acquired by a major e-commerce group and is proud to carry its part of the mission to 'make it easy to do business anywhere in the era of the digital economy'. As part of the global ecosystem, Nadia Store is leveraging global leadership and experience in technology, online commerce, mobile payment, and logistics to drive growth in its markets.
