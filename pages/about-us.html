About Daraz Store

Your trusted partner in quality products and exceptional service

Who We Are

Daraz Store is the leading online marketplace in South Asia, empowering tens of thousands of sellers to connect with millions of customers. We provide immediate and easy access to 10 million products in more than 100+ categories and deliver more than 2 million packages every month to all corners of our countries.

Daraz Store is a mall, marketplace, and community for its customers. It is also a university for entrepreneurs, and every month we educate more than 5,000 new sellers on e-commerce operations.

To overcome the logistics challenges in our market, Daraz Store has built its own logistics company specifically designed for e-commerce operations - Daraz Express (known as DEX) - to raise the standards for the industry. We are also helping existing and new logistics providers become digitized.

In 2018, Daraz Store was acquired by a major e-commerce group and is proud to carry its part of the mission to 'make it easy to do business anywhere in the era of the digital economy'. As part of the global ecosystem, Daraz Store is leveraging global leadership and experience in technology, online commerce, mobile payment, and logistics to drive growth in its markets.
