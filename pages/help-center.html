How to Track Your Order

Follow these simple steps to track your order and stay updated on its delivery status.

Step-by-Step Guide

1
Log into Your Account

Sign in to your Daraz Store account using your email and password.

2
Go to My Orders

Navigate to the "My Orders" section in your account dashboard.

3
Select Your Order

Click on the order you want to track from your order history.

4
View Tracking Details

See real-time updates about your order's location and delivery status.
